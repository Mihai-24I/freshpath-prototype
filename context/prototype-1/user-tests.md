**Why these scores** (times are in each user's edited video)

- **Find a display:** Users 2 and 3 found it alone in seconds (U2 00:22, U3 00:35). User 1 was redirected and the screen was explained to them; User 4 found it in the task list (00:19) but then got a hint to look at the displays.
- **Top 5 alternatives:** everyone reached the list, but the reason screen (“What's wrong at C?”) and the loading screen slowed all five. Users 1 and 2 were told to press “Skip to results”, User 4 was told “go further”, and User 3 probably heard “it's loading”.
- **Best alternative and why:** all five trusted the “Best match” label. User 2 read out the reasons fluently (02:39–03:01) and User 4 used the details as the answer for the manager (02:35). User 3 first read the avocado information and needed help (“Partially, let's say”, 02:13). User 1 did not find the reasons; the interviewer called it a failed task (06:01).
- **Complete a refill:** Users 2 and 4 went through it alone. Users 1 and 3 needed an explanation of the checklist or were told the photo is optional.
- **Change a product:** User 1 managed alone (11:04–11:29). The others hesitated about where to change it or what was selected (U2 04:30–04:38, U3 04:38, U4 04:08).
- **Prevent food waste:** Users 1, 3 and 4 found the expiring product in Storage quickly (U1 13:19, U3 06:52, U4 05:35). User 2 found it too, but no suggested display was shown, and the interviewer explained the intended design (07:16).

## Test feedback and insights

The testers trusted the app's “Best match” and found expiring stock easily. They got stuck on in-between screens, on the reasons screen, and on not knowing whether an action had worked.

### Patterns across testers

- **Unclear whether an action worked or is finished (all five).** U1: “this still says avocados?” (07:33). U3: “They replaced the avocados, right?” (03:48), and clicked Storage again because they thought it hadn't worked (07:20). U4: “Is it okay?” (04:38). With U2, the interviewer called Task 4 done before the tester had taken the photo (03:58).
- **The Task 2 path has extra steps.** The reason screen and the loading screen needed help for Users 1 and 2. U4 on the reason screen: “It's not an important step” (01:31).
- **“Best match” is trusted more than the reasons are read (all five).** U1: “because it was the best alternative” (04:24). U4: “I see the cherry tomatoes with Best match on it” (02:17). Only U2 read out several reasons without help (02:39–03:01).
- **The reasons screen is hard to read (Users 1 and 3).** U1: “it feels a bit cluttered” (05:37) and “the squares kind of confuse me” (18:26). U3: “you just have to read the summary as well to understand it all” (02:19).
- **Changing a product by hand is unclear (Users 2, 3 and 4).** U3: “Is it possible to change it?” (04:38), later “one of the hardest ones” (07:58). U2 expected a more direct route and search (04:30, 04:38). U4 was unsure what was selected: “Can I click…?” (04:08).
- **Buttons that look or act the same.** U2: “You have “Find alternatives” and “Edit”. These buttons essentially do the same thing” (05:23–05:27). U4: “have a primary button and a secondary button, because right now the style is the same” (06:09–06:11).
- **Storage and the red “best before” cue work (Users 1, 3 and 4).** U1: “it's outlined in red, so it's very easy to see” (13:19). U4: “strawberries… are best before today, so I would press on Suggest display” (05:35). The step after “Suggest display” is missing in the prototype (U2 07:16, 08:14).
- **First-time users need guidance (Users 1 and 3).** U1: “I think it's a bit difficult to get used to” (09:04) and “it's a bit too much text” (15:44). U3 suggested “an FAQ section” (08:19) or “a short tutorial” (09:17), and said “not many people like reading, including me” (05:41).
- **The photo step raises questions (Users 2, 3 and 4).** U3: “Why do I have to attach a photo?” (03:32). U4 noted it as optional (03:30). U2 nearly skipped it: “Oh wait, I'm not done yet” (04:02).

### Other feedback (one tester)

- U2: show the tasks on the overview, “Make it yellow because it's low, make it red because it's empty” (09:54); move Profile to the end (08:35); add sorting to Storage by best before, quantity and promotion (10:33); big buttons, because work phones break (05:52).
- U1: “Maybe there can be more colour, like images too” (20:15); the reasons screen title could be bolder (18:05).
- U2 wants to choose the refill amount: “I don't want it full” (03:33).

### Positive remarks

- U1: “The interactive aspect was really nice” (18:58).
- U3: “I think it's quite a decent app, to be honest” (10:35).
- U4: “I think the flow is understandable” (06:00).

## Conclusion

Prototype 1 shows the idea is understood: every tester found the problem display or the expiring stock and trusted the app's pick. The part meant to back up the employee's choice, the reasons, only worked without help for half the testers, and every flow lacks a clear “done” moment.

**What worked**

- Status cues on the overview and in Storage: “low”, colour, and the red best-before outline.
- The “Best match” label: all five used it to choose.
- The step-by-step refill for testers who went through it alone (Users 2 and 4).

**What confused the testers**

- In-between screens in Task 2: the reason screen and the loading screen.
- Finding and reading the reasons: too much text, rating squares, and needing the summary to understand it.
- No feedback after saving or replacing, and no screen after “Suggest display”.
- Where to change a product by hand, and buttons with the same style that seem to do the same thing.

**What to change for the next prototype**

| Change | Based on |
| --- | --- |
| Add confirmation states (“Display C updated”) and an updated overview after every action | All five testers |
| Remove or shorten the reason screen and auto-skip the loading screen | Help needed for Users 1 and 2; U4 01:31 |
| Put a short summary of the reasons on top, with less text, a clear title and consistent ratings | U1 05:37, 18:05, 18:26; U3 02:19 |
| One clear route to change a product: one primary button, search in the product list | U2 05:23, U3 04:38, U4 06:09 |
| Build the screen that highlights the suggested display after “Suggest display” | U2 07:16 |
| Explain why the photo is needed, or mark it as optional | U3 03:32, U4 03:30 |
| Add a short first-use guide or help section | U1 09:04, U3 08:19 |
